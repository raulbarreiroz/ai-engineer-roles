# Acceptable Use Policy (excerpt)

## Prohibited uses

Client shall not use the Services to:

1. Process payment card data unless a PCI SOW is signed.
2. Train foundation models on Provider Confidential Information without written consent.
3. Circumvent rate limits or authentication controls.

## Support hours

Standard support is Monday-Friday 09:00-18:00 America/Bogota, excluding local holidays. Severity-1 incidents receive a first response within **four (4) hours** during support hours.
