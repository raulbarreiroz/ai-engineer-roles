# Master Services Agreement (excerpt)

## 1. Parties

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between **Northwind Analytics LLC** ("Provider") and the client entity listed in an Order Form ("Client").

## 2. Services

Provider will deliver data engineering and analytics consulting as described in each Statement of Work (SOW). Work outside an SOW requires a written change order.

## 3. Fees and payment

Invoices are issued monthly in arrears. Payment is due within **thirty (30) days** of invoice date. Late amounts accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum allowed by law, whichever is lower.

## 4. Intellectual property

Pre-existing materials remain owned by the creating party. Work product created specifically for Client under a SOW is assigned to Client upon full payment, except for Provider's reusable frameworks, templates, and tooling ("Background IP").

## 5. Confidentiality

Each party shall protect the other's Confidential Information with reasonable care for **three (3) years** after disclosure, or longer for trade secrets while they remain secret.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate for convenience with **sixty (60) days** prior written notice. Either party may terminate for material breach if uncured within fifteen (15) days of notice.
